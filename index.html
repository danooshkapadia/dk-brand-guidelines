Visually powerful.Intellectually elegant.Human.

02 · System

Choose who talks.

Each page has one job. The design follows the thing carrying the meaning.

01 / TYPEStatement

Few words stop the reader. Covers, dividers, posters, title slides.

02 / WORDSWorking

A reader can follow an argument. Proposals, reports, web pages, deck interiors.

03 / PICTUREExplaining

A relationship becomes visible. Field guides, diagrams, teaching slides.

Register 01 / Statement03

Think in systems.Poster scale is earned. It does not belong on every page.
Register 02 / Working04
Words talk

Help the reader move.

Give the page a visible route through the idea.

01 / A point

Start with the decision the reader needs to make.

Use a strong heading, readable sentences, and space between steps. Let rules organize the work. Use a table when people need to compare, and a picture when the relationship matters more than the words.

What belongs here

Proposals, reports, interior slides, websites, and working documents.

Register 03 / Explaining05
Picture talks

Draw the relationship.

One framework. One editorial metaphor. The drawing should make the idea easier to see.

Make the handoff visible.

A picture can show ownership moving between people faster than a paragraph can explain it. The headline states the claim; the illustration proves it.

Image prompt: Flat vector editorial illustration on soft paper. Show one adult passing a clearly marked task to another; a smaller off-white robot supports the handoff. Use one red mark at the moment ownership changes. Smooth matte shapes, economical contours, no texture or text. Leave the upper-left open for a headline.

06 · Color

Three places to stand.

White is for work. Khaki marks a threshold. Black is a statement.

White#FFFFFF / WORK
Khaki paper#ECE3D7 / THRESHOLD
Black#000000 / STATEMENT

Soft paper is an aside in Working pages and may be a page ground in Explaining. Utility grey belongs in tables. Neither becomes a fourth general-purpose ground.

08 · Type

Give each voice its job.

Helvetica signals. Century reads. Mono names data. Caveat feels written by hand.

The grid10
10 · Layout

Make space do work.

Twelve columns give the page a repeatable skeleton, while the content chooses its shape.

010203040506070809101112

Outer margin: 5% of the short side. Gutter: 2% of width. Rule weights: 0.5, 1, and 2px.

Icons11
11 · Wayfinding

Use one icon family.

Quiet signs help readers move. They should never compete with the idea.

Read a note
Mark time

Working icons use an inline 24-unit grid, 1.5 stroke, square ends, and the current text color. Drawn Explaining details belong inside the illustration.

Handwriting12
12 · Human trace

Write where a person would.

Caveat belongs in a margin, a quote, or a whiteboard inside an image.

What changed?

The publication speaks in Helvetica and Century. Handwriting belongs to depicted thought, never to navigation or a long paragraph. Image prompts use the same single description so the drawn and typeset worlds agree.

Illustration and slots13
13 · Image making

Prompt the picture. Keep the slot.

The prompt states the claim, the metaphor, the actors, palette, red event, aspect ratio, and space reserved for text.

01 / FRAMING

Name the relationship.

One framework and one metaphor give the image a reason to exist.

02 / FORM

Keep shapes smooth.

Flat matte color, economical contours, expressive but stylized people.

03 / HANDOFF

Leave the image slot visible.

It stays labeled with its full prompt until an approved PNG arrives.

How rules work15
The operating rule

Know what can bend.

A useful system tells its makers where judgment belongs.

01 / MUST

Guardrails

Font roles, grounds, red, logo positions, readability floors, and the things that visibly break the brand.

02 / USUAL

Defaults

Registers, ratios, grid, meta row, icons and spacing. Depart with a clear reason.

03 / CHOOSE

License

Composition, scale within range, metaphor, crop and the one red point need a designer’s judgment.
